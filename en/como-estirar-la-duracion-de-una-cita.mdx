---
title: "How to stretch the length of an appointment"
---

Sometimes you realize on the go that an appointment needs more time than was booked, or the other way around: it was given more time than it will actually use. OCLÜ lets you adjust an appointment's length directly on the grid, by dragging its bottom edge down (to make it longer) or up (to make it shorter). It's the quickest way to make this adjustment without opening the details.

## Before you start

- The appointment has to already exist on the calendar.
- The new time range has to fall within the clinician's working hours. If, when you stretch the appointment, the end time lands in a blocked area (diagonal lines) or outside the working day, the system won't let you drop the edge there.
- If the time you want to extend the appointment into is taken by another appointment, the system will only allow the extension if the clinician or the treatment room has overlapping appointments (double-booking) enabled. Otherwise, it will reject the change.

## How to get to this section

1. Log in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the calendar icon to open the **Daily view**.
3. Go to the day of the appointment you want to lengthen or shorten.

## Steps to stretch the length

### 1. Find the appointment on the grid

Look for the appointment's colored block in the clinician's column. The appointment covers its current time range (for example, 11:00 to 11:30 is two half-hour cells).

### 2. Put the cursor on the bottom edge

Move the mouse cursor to the bottom edge of the appointment's block. When you're on the edge, the cursor changes to a resize icon (a vertical double arrow).

> Tip: if the cursor doesn't change, move it a few pixels up or down until you find the sensitive area. The active edge is a narrow strip at the bottom of the block.

### 3. Drag down (to lengthen) or up (to shorten)

Click and hold down the mouse button. Drag:

- **Down** to extend the length. The block grows and shows you what the new end time will be as you drag.
- **Up** to shorten the length. The block gets smaller.

OCLÜ shows you a live preview of the new block while you drag. The grid usually has a minimum step of 15 or 30 minutes, so the change "snaps" to those intervals.

Release the mouse button when the block is the length you want.

### 4. Confirm the change

When you release it, a **Change this appointment's duration** window opens ("Review the change before confirming it.") with a **Notify the patient about the change of time** checkbox. It's the same window that appears when you drag an appointment to another time (see the **How to reschedule an appointment by dragging it** guide); only the title changes. Tick the checkbox if you want to let the patient know, and confirm with **Yes, move it**.

If the new length clashes with another appointment or a block, you'll see a warning and the appointment will go back to its original size.

## Expected result

The appointment now has its new length. The start time stays the same; what changes is the end time. The patient only sees the change if you ticked the notify checkbox when confirming.

## Notes and variations

- **Only the end time changes, not the start time.** If you want to change the start, drag the whole appointment (see the **How to reschedule an appointment by dragging it** guide) or edit it from the details.
- **When this method works best:** quick adjustments during the day, when the visit has already started and you need to set aside more time, or when you're organizing the schedule at the end of the day.
- **When it's better to edit from the details:** if you need to change the start time, move it to another date, change other fields, or leave a note about why it changed.
- **If the appointment won't stretch:** most likely there's another appointment or a block in the target time. Check the grid and, if you need to overlap, enable overlapping appointments in the clinician's settings (Administration → Staff → Schedule settings) or the treatment room's settings (Administration → Clinics → Treatment room settings).
- **Shortening an appointment that has already started:** you can shorten it even after the visit has begun. The system doesn't stop you from shortening it to the current time or earlier, but check that it makes clinical sense (you don't want to "erase" time the patient was already being seen).
- **Permissions:** whether you can change appointment lengths depends on the permissions assigned to the staff member under Administration → Staff → Permissions. If the cursor doesn't change to the double arrow when you pass over the edge, check your settings.
