---
title: "How to hide and show amounts in Finances"
---

The main Finances dashboard holds a lot of sensitive information: cash account balances, the month's sales, outstanding balances, totals. If you work with the screen visible from the front desk or with a patient next to you, you don't always want the amounts on display. OCLÜ has a quick button that covers every value with one click, and shows them again when you need them. This guide shows you how to use it.

## When to use it

- When you're going over financial reports and a patient walks up to the desk.
- When you're going to share your screen in a meeting and don't want to show specific values.
- When you take screenshots of the system for documentation or support.
- When you work at a counter with the screen visible to the public.

## How to get to this section

1. Sign in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the Finances icon.

You'll land on the module's main dashboard, where you can see the monthly summary, the cash and bank accounts, the charts and the shortcuts.

## Steps to hide the amounts

1. On the main Finances dashboard, look at the top right of the screen.
2. You'll see a button with an **eye** icon that says **Hide amounts** (when the values are visible) or **Show amounts** (when they're already hidden).
3. Click the button.

**When the amounts are hidden:** every monetary value on the dashboard is replaced with dots, like this: `●●●●●●`. The labels (account names, categories, etc.) are still visible, but the numbers aren't.

**When the amounts are visible:** the dashboard shows all the values as usual.

Click again to go back to the previous state.

## Expected result

The main dashboard changes instantly. The cards (Balance, Cash and bank accounts, Insurer receivables, Clinician payments, etc.) show or hide their values depending on the button's state.

## Important: it only affects the main dashboard

The **Hide amounts / Show amounts** button works on the main dashboard of the Finances module. The detailed sub-views (Income, Expenses, Balance, Reports) always show the amounts, because there you're working with the data in detail and it wouldn't make sense to have them covered.

If you also want to hide those details, close the sub-view and go back to the main dashboard before showing the screen.

## Tips and variations

- **Suggested default for clinics with an exposed screen:** always leave the dashboard with the amounts hidden when you sign out, so the next person who signs in sees them covered from the start.
- **For team meetings:** if you're going to share your screen with the whole team, it's better to have the amounts visible. If the meeting is with a supplier or someone from outside, it's better to hide them.
- **It's not the same as limiting permissions:** this button is purely cosmetic, not a security measure. If you want some staff members not to be able to see the amounts, set that up from Administration → Staff → Permissions.

## Estimated time

1 second (one click).

## Permissions

Any staff member with access to the Finances module can use this button. It doesn't require a specific permission — it's just a visual control.
