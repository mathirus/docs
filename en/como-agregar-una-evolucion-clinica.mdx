---
title: "How to add a clinical note"
---

Clinical notes are the written record of what happened at each of the patient's visits. OCLÜ has a rich text editor for entering them, with formatting options, voice dictation, help from OCLÜ AI, file attachments and an optional link to a treatment plan. Once saved, clinical notes stay in the patient's clinical record and can be exported to PDF. This guide shows you the full flow.

## When to use it

- Right after the visit, to record what was done in the session.
- When you're going to refer the patient to another clinician and want to leave the clinical context.
- When you're documenting a routine check-up, post-treatment instructions or an observation about the patient.
- When the receptionist enters what the clinician dictated at the end of the day (assigning the clinical note to the clinician, not to themselves).

## Prerequisites

- The patient must already be in the system.
- If you're going to link the clinical note to a treatment plan, the treatment plan must already exist.

## How to get to this section

1. Log in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the Patients icon.
3. Find the patient and click their name to open their record.
4. In the record, click the **Clinical record** tab.
5. In the submenu on the left, click **Clinical notes**.

> In the breadcrumb at the top you'll see **Home → Patients → \[Patient name\]**.

## Steps to add a clinical note

### 1. Click Add clinical note

On the Clinical notes screen, you'll see:

- A counter **Clinical notes · N clinical notes**, with an info icon next to it.
- Two buttons at the top right: **Add clinical note** (black) and **Export PDF** (gray).
- Below them, a **Newest first / Oldest first** toggle to sort the list.
- A search box (**Search in the notes**) and two filter dropdowns: **All clinicians** and **All treatment plans**.
- Clinical notes are shown as cards (not as a table): each one with its date, the user who entered it, the name of the treatment plan or module it's linked to (if any), and the content or the status of the related treatment.

Click the black **Add clinical note** button.

### 2. Fill in the form

The **Add clinical note** window opens with the following fields:

**Visit date** (required): comes preselected with today's date. You can change it if you're entering a clinical note after the fact.

**Linked treatment plan** (optional): a dropdown to link this clinical note to a specific treatment plan of the patient's. If the visit was part of a treatment plan, link it. If it was a general visit with no treatment plan, leave it empty.

> Linking it to the treatment plan lets you later filter the clinical notes for a specific treatment and see the clinical progress in chronological order.

**Visit note** (required): the body of the clinical note. It's a rich text editor with a toolbar. Each feature is explained further down.

**Attachments** (optional): you can upload X-rays, clinical photos, scanned prescriptions, or any relevant file. Click **Attach files** and choose them from your computer.

**Signatures** (optional): two separate toggles.

- **Clinician's signature** — stamped with the date and time, in the name of whoever is entering the clinical note.
- **Patient's signature** — **Sign here** (the patient signs on the spot, on the screen) or **Send link** (so they can sign from their own phone).

**Follow-up** (optional): **Follow-up reminder**, with the **Schedule** button — schedules a reminder to book this patient again later (for example, a check-up in 30 days).

### 3. Use the Visit note editor tools

The editor's top bar has the following buttons, from left to right:

- **B** (Bold): select text and click it, or turn it on from the button before typing.
- **I** (Italic): same as bold, but in italics.
- **S** (Strikethrough): strikes through the text.
- **U** (Underline): underlines the text.
- **H1, H2, H3:** headings at three levels. Useful for organizing long clinical notes into sections.
- **P:** normal text (paragraph). Use it to go back to a paragraph after a heading.
- **Alignment:** aligns the text left, center or right.
- **Numbered list:** creates a 1, 2, 3... list.
- **Undo / Redo (arrows):** undo and redo changes.
- **Microphone (Record with voice):** click it and start talking. OCLÜ transcribes what you say straight into the editor. Useful when your hands are busy or you'd rather dictate than type. _It's an add-on: an additional service, not included in the plan by default (see the **Add-ons** page)._
- **Oclü AI:** an assistant that helps you write up the text you've already typed.

### 4. (Optional) Attach files

If you want to add files to the clinical note (X-rays, clinical photos, etc.), click **Attach files** below the editor and select the files from your computer.

### 5. Save the clinical note

When you're done, click the black **Add** button at the bottom right of the window.

OCLÜ saves the clinical note and the list of clinical notes updates with the new entry.

Clinical notes are kept in chronological order (adjustable with the **Newest first / Oldest first** toggle) and become a permanent part of the patient's clinical record.

## Exporting clinical notes to PDF

If you need a document with all of the patient's clinical notes (for a referral, to give to the patient, for an expert assessment):

1. On the Clinical notes screen, click the **Export PDF** button at the top right (next to Add clinical note).
2. OCLÜ generates a PDF with all the clinical notes entered, in chronological order, with the date, clinician and full content.
3. The PDF downloads in your browser.

## Common errors

**I don't see the field to assign the clinical note to another clinician:** you don't have that permission. Ask your administrator to grant it. By default, the clinical note is assigned to the staff member who created it.

**The microphone doesn't work:** check that your browser has microphone permission for the OCLÜ site. The first time you click the icon, the browser may ask you for permission.

**OCLÜ AI doesn't respond:** AI assistance may have a limited quota or be under maintenance. If it doesn't respond, write the clinical note by hand and try the AI again later.

**I uploaded a file but it doesn't show up:** check its size and format. OCLÜ accepts common formats (JPG, PNG, PDF, etc.), but very large files may fail to upload. Reduce the size and try again.

**I linked the clinical note to the wrong treatment plan:** edit the clinical note from its card (pencil icon) and change the linked treatment plan. Then save.

**I entered a clinical note and need to delete it:** from the clinical note's card, use the red delete icon. It asks for confirmation before applying.

**I can't find an old clinical note:** use the **Search in the notes** box, or the **All clinicians** / **All treatment plans** filters to narrow down the list.

## Tips and variations

- **Dictate instead of typing:** if you have the voice dictation add-on, it's faster than writing, especially between patients.
- **Use headings in long clinical notes:** H1/H2/H3 make a clinical note with several parts (history, examination, plan) easy to read later.
- **Always link to the treatment plan when it applies:** it lets you later filter by **All treatment plans** and see the clinical progress of a specific treatment without mixing it with the rest of the clinical record.
- **Patient's signature for informal acknowledgments:** if you need the patient to confirm something specific (not a formal consent form), the signature on the clinical note can serve as quick backup.

## Estimated time

1 to 3 minutes per clinical note, depending on how much detail you enter and whether you use voice dictation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit a clinical note after saving it?** Yes, from its card with the pencil icon. The system doesn't keep later edits as a separate change history.

**Can the patient see their own clinical notes?** It depends on how the patient portal is set up at your clinic. Check with your administrator whether your patients have that access enabled.

**Can I enter a clinical note without linking it to any treatment plan?** Yes, the Linked treatment plan field is optional. That's for general visits that aren't part of a specific treatment plan.

**What happens if I schedule a Follow-up reminder?** An internal staff reminder is created for that patient, on the date you choose. It isn't an automatic message to the patient.

## Permissions

Adding, editing and deleting clinical notes, as well as exporting them to PDF, requires specific permissions assigned under Administration → Staff → Permissions. If you don't see the **Add clinical note** button or some field in the form, check with your administrator.
