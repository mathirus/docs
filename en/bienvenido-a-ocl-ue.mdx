Welcome to **OCLÜ**, the management system designed to support the daily work of your dental clinic. From here you'll be able to manage patients, appointments, treatment plans, treatments and payments in a clear, organized way, with all the information centralized and accessible to every member of your team.\
_This guide is designed so you can use the system with confidence, understanding what each module does and how to get the most out of it in the clinic's daily routine._

---

## Who is OCLÜ for?

**OCLÜ** is designed for every role involved in the daily work of a dental clinic.

- **Dentists and specialists** — Access the patient's clinical information, record diagnoses, treatments and treatment plans, and follow up on each case.
- **Receptionists and front desk** — Manage the calendar, appointments and patients, supporting daily care and keeping the administrative information organized.
- **Administration** — Keep track of collections, payments and financial movements, with clear information for the clinic's financial control.
- **Assistants and support staff** — Help enter and look up information, documents and images, making it easier to keep the patient's data organized.

**Access and permissions are configurable**: each clinic decides what each user can see and do in the system, adapting OCLÜ to the way it works.

---

## How is OCLÜ designed?

**OCLÜ** was designed from clinical practice, not from a software logic. Each module of the system reflects the real way a dental clinic works day to day.

- **Easy to use** — Navigation and screens are organized so that every task can be done clearly and in order, without technical knowledge or unnecessary steps.
- **Centralized information** — All the patient's and the clinic's information is linked in one place, avoiding duplicates and making clinical and administrative follow-up easier.
- **Supports the patient throughout their journey** — From the first contact and booking the appointment, to the treatment, the follow-up and the related administrative side.
- **Adapted to each clinic** — Each clinic can set up users and permissions according to its internal organization, making sure each person only accesses what they need.
- **Secure and organized** — Access to the system is personal and controlled, keeping information protected for daily use.

## OCLÜ plans

**OCLÜ** offers different plans designed to adapt to the reality and pace of work of each dental clinic.\
The plans let you organize your use of the system according to the number of people working at the clinic, the treatment rooms available and the volume of patients, always keeping the same user experience and the same way of working. Whatever plan you have, **OCLÜ** keeps a clear, organized structure, where clinical and administrative information is integrated and available according to the permissions each clinic defines.\
The active plan determines which features are available and how much capacity the system has, and it can be adjusted over time if the clinic grows or changes the way it works.

## CORE

**An essential plan for small practices looking for order, simplicity and clear management from day one.**\
The **CORE** plan is designed for small practices or clinics that are taking their first steps in organizing their work digitally.\
It's ideal for those who want to start organizing patient information, appointments and daily tasks without complications, keeping a clear structure from the start. With this plan, the clinic can work with a small number of users and treatment rooms, which lets it centralize basic clinical and administrative information in one place.\
**CORE** supports the practice's day-to-day work, helping to organize the calendar, patients and the documents needed for care, while keeping a simple, orderly workflow.\
It's a good option for clinics that put clarity, control and ease of use first in their daily operations.

---

## STUDIO

**A plan designed for growing clinics that need more capacity, more users and a well-established organization of their daily work.**\
The **STUDIO** plan is aimed at growing clinics with more daily activity that need to expand their working capacity within the system.\
This plan lets you work with more users and treatment rooms, making it easier to organize the team and handle a larger volume of patients.\
**STUDIO** offers a broader structure for coordinating clinical and administrative tasks, keeping all the information integrated and accessible to the clinic's different roles.\
It's especially useful for clinics where several clinicians and administrative staff are involved, and where having clear, up-to-date, shared information is essential. This is the plan most often chosen by clinics that already have a stable way of working and want to consolidate their internal organization without losing simplicity.

---

## SIGNATURE

**An advanced plan for clinics with large structures that need more control, customization and a complete view of how they operate.**

The **SIGNATURE** plan is designed for clinics with larger structures or with advanced organization and control needs.\
It's designed for large teams, with multiple treatment rooms and a high level of daily activity, where clear management of information is key to how the clinic runs.\
**SIGNATURE** lets you work with a larger number of users and gives access to extended features, supporting both clinical and administrative management in an integrated way.\
This plan offers a higher level of customization and support, adapting to clinics that need a more complete and detailed view of their daily operations.\
It's an option aimed at clinics looking for a high level of organization, control and follow-up of their work, always keeping the clinical logic that characterizes **OCLÜ**.

---

## CUSTOM

**For dental businesses that go beyond the limits of any of the standard plans**. It includes multiple locations, a tailored number of treatment rooms and users, and closer support from the **OCLÜ** team.\
Ideal for clinic chains or dental groups that need a solution designed specifically for their operations.
