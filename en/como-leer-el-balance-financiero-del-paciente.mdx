---
title: "How to read the patient's financial Balance"
---

The **Balance** tab in the patient's record shows you the full history of financial movements: the treatments the patient was charged for, the payments you received, the voided payments. It's the screen you go to whenever you want to know how much a patient owes, what they paid and when. This guide explains how to read each column and what each type of movement means.

## When to use it

- When a patient asks how much they owe or how much they've paid.
- When you need to see the receipt for a specific payment.
- When you want to void a payment (see the **How to void a payment** guide).
- When you're doing a financial audit of a patient.
- When you want to see whether a patient has a credit balance or a debt in a specific currency (ARS or USD).

## How to get to this section

1. Sign in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the Patients icon.
3. Find the patient and click their name.
4. In the record, click the **Balance** tab (the last one, to the right of Treatment plans).

## What the screen shows

You'll see a table with all of the patient's movements in chronological order (most recent at the top). It has a movement search and the following columns:

- **Date:** when the movement happened.
- **Detail:** a description of the movement. It can be:
  - The name of a treatment that was charged (e.g. "Tratamiento de Conducto Unirradicular", "Composite Nanohíbrido Anterior"). It appears in color and sometimes as a clickable link to the treatment plan.
  - A green **"Payment received"** tag when the patient paid something.
  - A struck-through **"Payment voided"** tag when a payment was canceled.
- **Amount:** the value of the movement.
  - Negative (red) when it's a debt (a treatment charged).
  - Positive (green) when it's a payment received.
  - Struck through when it's a voided payment (it doesn't affect the balance).
- **Exchange rate:** if the movement involves two currencies (for example a payment in USD applied to a treatment plan in ARS), the exchange rate used appears here. If there's no conversion, it shows a dash.
- **User:** the staff member who recorded the movement. For some payments it may be blank if the payment predates tracking.
- **ARS balance:** the patient's running balance in pesos up to that movement.
- **USD balance:** the running balance in dollars.
- **Actions:** two icons on each payment row.

## The types of movement, one by one

### 1. Treatment charged (debt)

It appears in red with a negative amount (for example "−\$400.000,00 ARS"). In the Detail column you'll see the name of the treatment the patient was billed for (sometimes as a link to the treatment plan it was charged to).

> This means: the patient was charged for the treatment but hasn't paid for it yet. It increases their debt.

### 2. Payment received

It appears with a green "Payment received" tag in the Detail column and a positive green amount (for example "\+\$400.000,00 ARS").

> This means: the patient paid that amount. It reduces their debt. If it's more than they owed, it remains as a credit balance.

### 3. Payment voided

It appears struck through in gray, with a "Payment voided" tag and the original payment amount also struck through.

> This means: that payment was canceled in the records. **It doesn't affect the patient's balance** (it's as if it never happened, but it stays visible for auditing). See the **How to void a payment** guide to understand the flow.

## Actions for each payment

In the **Actions** column on the right, each **payment received** has two icons:

- **Document icon (View receipt):** opens the payment receipt in a new window or downloads it. Useful for giving it to the patient or filing it.
- **Circle with an X icon (Void):** starts the voiding flow. It asks for a reason and the voiding is recorded. There's a dedicated guide for this flow.

Both icons are neutral gray — they don't have different colors.

> A payment **can't be voided** if fees have already been paid out to the clinician based on it. In that case, contact support.

> **Treatments charged** (red rows) and **voided payments** (struck through) don't have these actions, because they aren't individually payable items.

## How to read the ARS balance and USD balance columns

The last two columns show you the patient's running balance up to that movement, split by currency:

- **ARS balance:** adds up all the movements in pesos.
- **USD balance:** adds up all the movements in dollars.

You'll see on each row how the balance changes:

- If the balance is negative (red), the patient owes money.
- If it's zero, they're up to date.
- If it's positive (green), they have a credit balance.

> If the patient has treatment plans in both currencies, look at both columns to understand their full situation. One can be at zero and the other in debt.

## How to find a specific movement

If the list is long, use the **movement search** at the top left. Type part of the name of the treatment or item and the list is filtered instantly.

## Expected result

The Balance tab gives you a complete, chronological view of the patient. For any financial question, this is the reference screen.

## Common errors

**I don't see the Actions icons on some rows:** the icons only appear on payment received rows. Treatments charged and voided payments aren't individual actions.

**The balance doesn't match what I expected:** check whether there are voided payments (struck through) that you're counting by mistake. Voided payments don't affect the final balance.

**The patient says they paid something that doesn't show up:** check the date. If the payment was entered by another staff member, it may be on a different date. If it doesn't show up at all, the payment wasn't recorded and needs to be entered.

**A USD balance shows up but the patient never paid in dollars:** check the patient's treatment plans. If they have a treatment plan in USD even though you've only entered payments in ARS, the USD balance shows the debt in that currency until you adjust it.

## Tips and variations

- **Before taking a payment from a patient:** open Balance to see whether they have a credit balance (it can be applied to the payment) or a previous debt (you add it to today's payment).
- **To hand out receipts in bulk:** filter by date or item if you need several receipts in a row. Each one is downloaded individually with the document icon.
- **Monthly audit:** open the Balance of patients you suspect have inconsistencies (for example, a very old debt with no movements). The full history helps you understand what happened.
- **If you're going to void several payments:** do them one at a time from the void icon. Voiding asks for a reason (at least 10 characters) each time.

## Estimated time

10 to 30 seconds to open and check a patient's balance. 1 minute if you're going to download a specific receipt.

## Permissions

Viewing the balance is usually available to all staff with access to the patient's record. The View receipt and Void payment actions depend on the permissions assigned in Administration → Staff → Permissions. If you don't see the actions icons, check with your administrator.
