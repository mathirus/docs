---
title: "How to customize the system's appearance and contact support"
---

OCLÜ has quick controls at the top and bottom of the screen to customize your experience and to ask for help when you need it. This guide shows you where each control is and what it's for.

## When to use it

- When you start using the system and want to adjust the view to your visual preference.
- When you work long hours at the screen and prefer dark mode to reduce eye strain.
- When you have a question and want to contact the support, sales or complaints team without leaving the system.
- When you want to open the OclüLearn academy or the User guide to learn more.

## The three-dot menu (top-right corner)

In the top-right corner of every OCLÜ screen, next to your user, you'll see a **three-dot** icon ("..."). Click it to open the personal settings and support menu.

The menu has five options:

### 1. Switch to dark mode / Switch to light mode

This is the first item in the menu. The text shows the action you're about to take, not the current state: if you're in light mode it says "Switch to dark mode", and vice versa.

- **Light mode:** white background, dark text. It's the default and works well in good natural light.
- **Dark mode:** dark background, light text. It reduces eye strain during long sessions or in low ambient light.

The change applies instantly across the whole interface. It doesn't affect other users; each staff member can have their own preference.

### 2. Language

The second item lets you choose the language you use the system in, such as English or Spanish. The change applies right away and only affects your user.

### 3. OclüLearn

The third item takes you to **OclüLearn**, OCLÜ's training academy. There you'll find videos, guides and resources to learn how to use the system, go deeper into specific modules or train a new staff member.

### 4. User guide

The fourth item takes you to this same written documentation, without leaving the system. It's useful when you have a specific question about how a screen works and would rather read about it right then than look for a video.

### 5. Contact

The fifth item opens a window with two WhatsApp numbers for writing to the team:

- **Sales, admin and support:** for general questions, administrative questions, or when you want to add add-ons or upgrade your plan.
- **Complaints:** for when you need to escalate a specific problem.

## Support button at the bottom of the screen

In addition to the top menu, at the bottom right of every OCLÜ screen you'll see a direct link to WhatsApp:

- **Support** (with a WhatsApp icon): opens WhatsApp so you can chat with the team when you have a problem with the system. It goes to the same "Sales, admin and support" number as the Contact window.

## When to use each channel

- **Mode / Language:** when you want to adjust the system's personal appearance.
- **OclüLearn:** when you're learning or training someone with video tutorials.
- **User guide:** when you want to read the written documentation without leaving the system.
- **Contact → Sales, admin and support** (or the **Support** button at the bottom of the screen): for general questions, technical problems or questions about your plan.
- **Contact → Complaints:** when you need to escalate a specific problem.

## Tips and variations

- **Try dark mode at least once:** if you've never used it, give it a full day. Many people end up preferring it and find that it reduces eye strain.
- **Team training:** when you bring a new member onto the staff, recommend that they start with OclüLearn before touching the system. It saves them a lot of learning time on live work.
- **Contacting support:** before you get in touch, try to reproduce the problem with details (what you were doing, what message you saw, on which screen). That lets the team solve it faster.

## Estimated time

1 to 2 seconds to change the mode or language. WhatsApp opens instantly for support, sales or complaints.

## Permissions

These controls are available to every staff member. They don't require any special permissions.
