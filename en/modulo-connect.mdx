title: "Connect"
---

The **Connect** module is the inbox where the clinic reads and replies to its patients' WhatsApp messages, with an optional virtual assistant that can handle chats on its own. You open it from the chat icon in the sidebar.

> **Connect is an add-on**: if your clinic hasn't subscribed to it, you won't see this icon in the menu.

---

## Conversation inbox

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-485.png)
</Frame>

This is the module's main screen. You can filter the conversation list by **All**, **Mine** or **Unassigned**, and also by **Tags** or by **Team member**. Each conversation shows whether it **Needs attention**, who it's assigned to, and how long it has been waiting for a reply.

From here you can also open **Reminders** (the internal reminders linked to this inbox) and start a **New conversation** with a number that hasn't written in yet.

### Inside a conversation

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-499.png)
</Frame>

When you open a conversation, the header lets you **assign** it to someone on the team, set a **reminder**, and turn the **AI** on or off for that chat only. If the virtual assistant isn't part of your subscription or is turned off for that task, a "Waiting for the team" notice appears, telling you the chat needs a manual reply.

The right panel shows the **patient** linked to the number (or a button to link one if it isn't linked yet) and an **Internal notes** area, visible only to the team — the patient never sees them.

From the reply field you can send emojis, attach files, record audio, mention another team member within the conversation, or **schedule** a message to be sent later.

## Settings

You open it with the gear icon inside Connect itself — it isn't in Administration. It's organized into four tabs.

### Getting started

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-498.png)
</Frame>

A step-by-step guide to getting Connect up and running: which location receives the WhatsApp messages, the linked official line, whether the clinic's WhatsApp Web is connected, who on the team handles the inbox, the out-of-hours notice, and the quick replies/tags/categories. A progress bar shows how many of the required steps are already done.

### AI

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-500.png)
</Frame>

Settings for the **virtual assistant** — a separate service within Connect, optional on top of the base add-on.

- **Identity and status** — the name it introduces itself with, whether it addresses the patient informally or formally, and when it's active (always, or only when the clinic is closed).
- **What it can do** — for each task (booking appointments, looking up the patient's appointments, canceling them, moving them to another day or time, handling new patients) you choose one of three modes: **Handles it alone**, **Notifies the team** (it flags the chat so someone can review it) or **Off** (it tells the patient to call the clinic).
- **Inbox team** — who on the team takes over when the AI hands off a conversation.

### Line and inbox

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-501.png)
</Frame>

- **WhatsApp line** — the **Official number** only lets you reply within 24 hours of the patient's last message; linking the clinic's **WhatsApp Web** (by scanning a code) lets you message a patient first or reply after that limit.
- **Outside opening hours** — an automatic notice (with customizable text) that goes out once per day per patient, when they write while the clinic is closed.
- **Auto-close** — handing the chat back to the AI after a set number of hours with no activity, and whether each message is signed with the name of whoever replies from the clinic.
- **Areas** — the options the patient sees when the AI offers to put them through to someone; each handed-off chat is assigned to one person and is never reassigned automatically.
- **Patients who asked not to receive messages** — the list of people who wrote "STOP" or something similar; they won't be messaged again.

### Tags and replies

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-502.png)
</Frame>

- **Quick replies** — predefined texts the team inserts from the chat with the Replies button.
- **Tags** — to mark conversations and find them later with the inbox filter.
- **Categories** — classify what each chat is about (appointments, treatment plans, complaints).

## If you don't see some of this

Connect is an add-on: if your clinic hasn't subscribed to it, the module doesn't appear in the sidebar. If you have subscribed and a screen or option is still missing, it may be a permissions issue — check with your clinic's administrator. If you still can't after that, write to OCLÜ support and we'll help you.
