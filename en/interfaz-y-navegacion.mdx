Once you log in to OCLÜ, the system takes you straight to the **home** screen, the main screen you'll use to run the clinic day to day. It's the first thing you see every time you open the system.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-467.png)
</Frame>

The OCLÜ interface is made up of three areas that are always there while you move around the system:

- **Left sidebar menu** — where you open every module
- **Header** — with quick links, notifications and profile settings
- **Central content area** — where the information for each module or view is shown

---

### Sidebar menu

The sidebar menu is on the left side of the screen and is always visible. By default it's collapsed, showing only each module's icon. When you hover over it, it expands and shows each module's full name.

When you click any module, the system takes you to that section and the item is highlighted in gray, showing you which part of the system you're in at that moment.

From the sidebar menu you open every module in the system: **Calendar, Patients, Laboratory, Stock, Finance, Connect and Administration.**

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-411.png)
</Frame>

**Connect** is the inbox where you read and reply to WhatsApp messages with the clinic's patients. It's an add-on: if your clinic hasn't purchased it, it doesn't appear in the sidebar menu. Its settings (the location that receives the WhatsApp messages, the linked line, the team with access) are managed from the gear icon inside Connect itself, not from Administration.

### Header

The header is fixed at the top of the screen and is visible from every module or section of the system. It contains the following items, from left to right:

**Clinic logo and name** Shows the logo you've uploaded to the system and the name of the clinic you're working in at that moment. If your user has access to more than one location, you can click the down arrow to switch clinics without logging out.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-467.png)
</Frame>

**Search** The magnifying glass icon opens a system-wide search. From there you can search for shortcuts to different sections of the system — for example, you can go straight to the outstanding balances report without clicking through module after module — and you can also search for patients. However, this search isn't designed specifically for patients, so it may take a few seconds longer than the search inside the Patients module. If you search for a patient from here and the result takes a while, it doesn't mean the patient isn't in the system; this search simply covers more ground and is slower at that specific task. For frequent or urgent patient searches, we recommend using the search in the Patients module.

**Notifications** The bell icon shows two kinds of information: any reminders you have pending, and all the system news, such as improvements, new features and updates the OCLÜ team keeps adding. It's the main channel we use to tell you about everything new that comes to the system.

### Home

The home screen is the system's main screen and the first thing you see when you log in. It's designed to give you a quick, complete view of what matters most today, without having to open each module separately. The information is organized into **islands**, which are independent blocks of content, each focused on a specific area of the clinic's work.

The islands come in an order set by the system, but you can rearrange them by dragging and dropping them wherever you like (using the six-dot icon to the left of each island's title). This layout is saved for your user.

---

#### Appointments island

This is the main island on the home screen. It shows the full list of today's appointments, with the following information for each one: time, patient name, linked treatment plan, appointment description and the appointment's current status.

If you have the right permissions, you can filter the list by clinician to see only one clinician's appointments. This is especially useful in clinics with several clinicians, where each one can check their own schedule from the home screen without opening the Calendar module.

  ![Image](/images/image-493.png)
</Frame>

#### Calendar island

It shows two key figures: the total number of appointments booked for today and the next appointment based on the current time. If the day's last appointment has already passed — for example, if the clinic closes at 6:00 p.m. and it's already 7:00 p.m. — this island won't show a next appointment until the following day.

#### Patients island

It shows today's birthdays, that is, which patients have their birthday today. When you click a patient's name:

- If there's only one birthday, the system takes you straight to that patient's profile.
- If there are two or more birthdays, a list opens with all the patients who have their birthday that day, and from there you can open each one's profile. Once inside the profile, you can contact the patient directly on WhatsApp from the header.

#### Finance island

It shows the clinic's monthly balance. A label tells you which month the amount shown belongs to. If you need to hide the figures for privacy — for example, if there are other people nearby — you can do it with this island's eye icon. Hiding them is temporary and only affects what you see.

#### Lab island

It shows how many lab requests are due for delivery today and which patients those requests belong to. When you click it, the system takes you straight to the Laboratory module so you can see the full details of each request.

#### Reminders island

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-496.png)
</Frame>

It's designed for managing pending tasks within the clinic team. It works as an internal reminder system so that no important task gets forgotten, whoever is responsible for carrying it out.

The list shows all pending reminders sorted by date, with the following information for each one: the date the task was due, the linked patient if there is one, the reminder's description, its category and the actions available.

**Actions available from the list:**

- **View detail** — opens a window with the reminder's full information, including the users who were notified.
- **Mark as done** — closes the reminder and moves it to the completed section.
- **Add a new reminder** — opens the form to create a new reminder.

**Form for creating a reminder:** When you create a new reminder, the system asks you to fill in the following fields:

- **Date** — when you want to be reminded of the task.
- **Category** — the categories available are the ones you've set up. The system comes with two preloaded categories: _Staff_ (for reminders assigned to team members) and _Personal_ (for each user's own reminders). Both can be edited, and you can create new categories to suit your clinic's needs.
- **Description** — the reminder's text, that is, the task or action that needs to be done.
- **Patient** (optional) — you can link a patient to the reminder as an internal reference. The patient doesn't get any notification; the link is only internal to the clinic.
- **Users to notify** — you can assign the reminder to yourself or to another staff member. If you don't select anyone, the reminder won't show up for any user.
- **Repeat** (optional) — when you turn this option on, the reminder becomes a recurring reminder. The system asks you how often you want it to repeat (for example, every 1 month, every 3 weeks, every 1 year) and until what date. If you want it to go on indefinitely, you can enter an end date far in the future.

Once you've filled in all the fields, click **Add reminder** to save it.

**Reminder settings:** The settings button inside the island takes you to a section with three tabs:

- **Pending reminders** — the full list of all active reminders.
- **Recurring reminders** — shows all the reminders that have repeat turned on. This feature is designed especially for reception and administration. A typical use case is a patient in orthodontic treatment who needs a monthly check-up: instead of creating a new reminder every month, the receptionist creates a recurring reminder linked to that patient and the system reminds them automatically every month until the date set. That takes away the mental load of having to remember to create it again.
- **Completed reminders** — a history of all the reminders that were marked as done. Useful for keeping track of closed tasks. From this view you can also delete reminders permanently.

#### Messages island

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-488.png)
</Frame>

It works as the clinic's internal messaging system, similar to a chat, but with features designed specifically for a clinical setting. One important difference from other messaging tools is that **messages can't be deleted**: all communication stays permanently recorded in the system, which ensures traceability and keeps important information from getting lost.

When you open the island, the system automatically filters unread messages so you see what you haven't checked yet first.

In the conversation list, each chat has an actions menu with an eye icon. Clicking it opens a preview window right on the home screen, without opening the messages module. From that window you can read the conversation and send a quick reply, or open it in full if you need more context. It's a way to keep up with messages without interrupting what you're doing on the home screen.

**Features available in messaging:**

- **Create a new chat** — when you do, the system asks you to enter a title for the conversation and select the participants. You can only add users who have access to the system, that is, members of the clinic's staff.
- **React with emojis** to individual messages within a conversation.
- **Reply to a specific message** — you can reply to one particular message within a conversation, similar to WhatsApp's reply feature.
- **Mark as unread** — to come back to a conversation later without losing it.
- **Add participants to an ongoing conversation** — you can bring a staff member into a conversation that's already active. This is useful, for example, when you're coordinating with the receptionist about a patient who is seen by another clinician: you can add that clinician to the conversation so everyone is in the loop without having to create a new chat.

Chats can't be archived or muted.

Messaging is also available in the **OCLÜ mobile app**, so the whole team can stay in touch even when they're away from the computer.

### Help resources always available

From anywhere in the system, at any time, you have access to two help resources:

### OCLÜ bot

It's in the bottom-left corner of the screen. It's the system's virtual assistant, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It draws on this user guide, so it can answer questions about how the system works, its modules and how to do different things in OCLÜ. When you open it, it greets you with: _"Hi! I'm the Oclü CRM assistant. How can I help you today?"_

### Play tutorial

It's in the bottom-right corner of the screen. In many modules and sections of the system you'll find this button, which starts an interactive step-by-step guide. When you click it, windows open that explain what you're looking at in that section and what each element is for. It's especially useful when you're exploring a module for the first time or when you want to better understand a specific feature. Not every module in the system has a tutorial available.
