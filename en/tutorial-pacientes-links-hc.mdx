OCLÜ has five tools that let patients interact with the system without you entering their details by hand: the **registration link** (so a new patient can enter their own details), the **medical background update link** (individual or bulk), the **clinical record PDF download** and **patient portal access**. This guide covers all five and shows you which one to use in each case.

## When to use it

- When a new patient is coming in for the first time and you want them to arrive with their details already entered.
- When you need to update the medical history of long-standing patients without calling them one by one.
- When you need to give the patient or another professional a PDF with their complete clinical record.
- When you want the patient to have ongoing access to their details, treatment plans and appointments from their phone.
- To cut down on front-desk time and avoid typos in sensitive data.

## The five tools at a glance

| Tool | What it's for | Who uses it | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Registration link | The new patient enters all their details | A patient with no profile in the system | Yes, 24-48 hours (single use) |
| Individual update link | A patient updates their medical background | A patient already in the system | Yes, 48 hours |
| Bulk update request | You ask several patients to update their medical background | A batch of patients with pending details | Yes (each individual link) |
| Clinical record PDF | Downloadable document with the clinical record | You download it to hand over | N/A |
| Patient link (portal) | Permanent patient access to their details | A patient already in the system | No (an account with email + password), but it counts against your plan's quota |

## Tool 1: Registration link for a new patient

This is the fastest way for a patient to register themselves before their first visit. You generate a unique link for that patient, along with a QR code that points to the same link, and you send it to them however you like.

### How to get there

1. In the left sidebar, click the Patients icon.
2. Click the black **Add patient** button at the top right.
3. In the dropdown that opens, choose **Share link**.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-470.png)
</Frame>

### Steps

1. The **Share registration link** window opens, with two modes: **Single use** (for one specific patient) or **Permanent** (reusable, to leave posted on social media or on the clinic's WhatsApp).
2. OCLÜ instantly shows you the link and a QR code, both pointing to the same public form.
3. Share the link or the QR code however you like:
   - **Copy the link:** paste it into WhatsApp, email, a text message or wherever you need it.
   - **Share the QR code:** show it on screen so the patient can scan it with their phone right there.
4. The patient opens the link (or scans the QR code), fills in the form (the same fields as the manual wizard: personal details, contact, address, health insurer) and submits it.
5. The patient's profile is added automatically to your Patients list, ready for you to book appointments.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-471.png)
</Frame>

> **Important:** in **Single use** mode, the link expires **7 days** after it's generated if the patient doesn't use it. In **Permanent** mode it doesn't expire.

## Tool 2: Individual update link

Use it when a patient is already in the system but their medical background is missing (or out of date) and you want them to fill it in themselves remotely.

### How to get there

1. In the Patients list, click the patient's name to open their profile.
2. In the **Personal details → Information** tab, look at the top bar of the fields section: you'll see two buttons, **Download** and **Update details**.

### Steps

1. Click **Update details**. The **Ask the patient for their details** window opens. It says you can generate a link so the patient can review their personal details and fill in their medical background from their phone, and that whatever they enter is saved directly to their profile. It has a **Generate link** button.
2. Click **Generate link**.
3. OCLÜ creates a unique link for that patient. It shows you the link, a **Copy** button and a **Send by email** button.
4. You can send it in two ways:
   - **Copy the link** and paste it wherever you like (WhatsApp, text message).
   - **Click Send by email** so OCLÜ automatically sends it to the email address on their profile.
5. When the patient opens the link, they fill in their personal details and medical background (Medical history, Medications, Dental history, Habits), and the information is updated directly on their profile.

> **Important:** this link expires 48 hours after it's generated. If the patient doesn't open it within that time, you'll have to generate it again.

## Tool 3: Bulk medical background update request

Useful for campaigns: asking every patient with pending medical background to update it.

### How to get there

1. In the left sidebar, click the Patients icon.
2. In the list, look at the top bar: there's a **Data update request** button (with an envelope icon).

### Steps

1. Click **Data update request**. The **Request medical background** window opens, with two counters at the top: how many patients have an email address and how many don't.
2. There are three ways to choose recipients:
   - **Search for specific patients** with the search box and tick them one by one.
   - **Tick the "X of Y selected" checkbox** to select every pending patient with an email address.
   - **Expand the "X patients with no email on file" section** to see which ones you can't reach by email (you'll have to ask them for it some other way).
3. Once they're selected, the **Send (X)** button at the bottom right shows how many you're going to contact. Click it to send.
4. OCLÜ sends an email to each selected patient with their own individual update link.

> Each link generated by this bulk request also expires after 48 hours. If the patient doesn't respond within that time, you'll have to include them in another request or generate an individual link.

## Tool 4: Download the clinical record PDF

To hand over the clinical record to the patient or another professional, or to keep as a backup.

### How to get there

1. In the Patients list, click the patient's name.
2. On their profile, in the **Personal details → Information** tab (also available under **Clinical record**), look at the top bar of the section.
3. The first button is **Download**.

### Steps

1. Click **Download**.
2. OCLÜ generates the PDF and downloads it in your browser.
3. The PDF is organized into sections: a header with the clinic's details, identification of the patient and the treating professional, medical background, reason for visit and initial clinical exam, progress notes and chronological records (date, professional, procedure and notes), diagnoses and treatments performed, prescribed medication (name, dose, route, frequency, duration, date and professional), complementary tests requested, and a discharge summary or case summary.

> If the clinic has the Periodontal chart or Facial aesthetics module, that information may also appear in the PDF. Exactly what's included depends on the system version and the active module.

## Tool 5: Patient link (patient portal)

This creates a user account for the patient with permanent access to the portal. It lets the patient see their appointments, treatment plans, prescriptions and much more from their phone or computer whenever they want.

### How to get there

1. Open the patient's profile.
2. In the profile header you'll see the **Patient link** button (with a chain icon).

### Steps

1. Click **Patient link**. The **Patient access** window opens.
2. If the patient doesn't have access yet, confirm to create the user. OCLÜ creates it and sends the patient an email with their login credentials (username and password) for the portal. Tell the patient to check their spam folder if they don't see it right away.
3. If the patient already has access, the same window shows a **Portal access** switch (to enable or disable it) and a **Resend** button to send them their credentials again.

### After creating access: "Manage patient access"

Once the user has been created, the same **Patient link** button is still the way in: when you click it again, the **Patient access** window now shows the switch and the credentials instead of the confirmation request. From there you can:

- **Enable or disable** the patient's access to the portal.
- **Resend the credentials** by email if the patient doesn't remember them.

> **Portal access counts against a quota in your plan.** From **My subscription** (the menu under your name, in the header) you can see how many accesses you've already given and how many you have left. If you run out, you can disable access for a patient who hasn't come in for a while and give it to a new one.

## Expected result

Depending on the tool:

- **Registration link:** a new profile in the Patients list, filled in by the patient themselves.
- **Update link (individual or bulk):** medical details updated directly on the patient's profile, with no manual work on your part.
- **Clinical record PDF:** a file downloaded to your computer, ready to send or print.
- **Patient link:** an active patient account with portal access, using one slot of your plan's quota.

## Common errors

**The update link expired before the patient used it:** individual links expire after 48 hours. Generate a new one from the patient's profile and send it again.

**The patient didn't get the bulk request email:** check that the email address on their profile is spelled correctly. Ask them to check their spam folder.

**I registered a patient with the registration link but their details are incomplete:** the public form asks for the same required fields as the manual wizard. If something is missing, check the profile and fill in what's missing (or send the update link again so the patient can finish it themselves).

**I created the patient portal but the patient didn't get the email with their credentials:** check the email address on the profile. If it's correct, ask them to check their spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive, you can resend the credentials from **Manage patient access**.

**I can't give portal access to a new patient:** check your plan's access quota from **My subscription**. If you've used it all, disable access for an inactive patient or get more quota.

**The clinical record PDF is incomplete:** the PDF reflects what's entered in the system. If something is missing, add it to the profile and download it again.

## Tips and variations

- **QR code face to face with the patient:** if the patient is at the front desk, show them the QR code on screen so they can scan it with their phone and start entering their details right away. Remember it's single use, so generate a new link for each patient.
- **Combine a bulk request with scheduled appointments:** if you have 20 annual checkups coming up next month, send the bulk medical background request a week before. Patients will arrive with everything filled in.
- **Before downloading the clinical record for a referral:** make sure the progress notes, medical background and odontogram are up to date. The PDF reflects the current state of the system.
- **Manage your portal quota wisely:** give access to patients who will really use it on an ongoing basis (long treatments, remote follow-up). For a one-off task, the update link is enough.

## Estimated time

- Generating and sending a registration link: 30 seconds.
- Generating an individual update link and sending it by email: 30 seconds.
- Bulk update request: 1 to 2 minutes to set up, depending on the number of patients.
- Downloading the clinical record PDF: 5 to 10 seconds.
- Creating patient portal access: 30 seconds.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the registration link be reused for several patients?** No. The link and the QR code are single use: each link lets only one patient enter their details. For each new patient you have to generate a new one from **Add patient → Share link**.

**How long does the registration link last before it expires?** It lasts for a limited time (between 24 and 48 hours). If the patient doesn't open it within that time, the link expires and you have to generate a new one from the same screen.

**Can the update link be reused after 48 hours?** No. Each time the patient needs it, you have to generate a new one from their profile (or include them again in a bulk request).

**What happens if the patient enters their details through the link and they already existed with those details?** If the system detects a match (by ID document, email or phone), it lets you know. Check manually whether it's the same patient or a new one.

**How many patients can have portal access at the same time?** It depends on your plan. Check your available quota from **My subscription**.

**What information does the patient see in the portal?** Dashboard, their appointments (pending, completed and next appointment), digital prescriptions, media, billing (account statement by currency) and, if applicable, their family group.

**Can I deactivate a patient's portal account?** Yes. From **Manage patient access** on the patient's profile you can disable access (and enable it again later) or resend the credentials.

## Permissions

Access to these tools (generating links, sending bulk update requests, creating portal users) depends on the permissions assigned to the staff member under Administration → Staff → Permissions. If you don't see some of the buttons described here, check with your administrator.
