---

Besides the base plan, OCLÜ offers **add-ons**: paid extra services that add a specific feature to the clinic. An add-on isn't included by default — you have to sign up for it separately — and until you do, the matching screen shows a promotional window explaining the service and a button to sign up for it, instead of the actual feature.

This page goes over what each add-on available today does and what you see once it's active at the clinic.

> To sign up for any of these services, message us on WhatsApp: use the **Support** button at the bottom of the screen, or the **Contact** option in the three-dot menu (top right, next to your user) and choose the **Sales, admin and support** number.

---

## WhatsApp Integration

Changes the channel for appointment reminders and confirmations: instead of (or as well as) sending them by email, they go out on WhatsApp with buttons so the patient can confirm or cancel right from the chat. Email stays as an automatic backup — it's only sent if the WhatsApp message fails (patient with no phone, invalid number or a template not yet approved by Meta).

With the add-on active, **Administration → Automations** shows the "Appointment reminder over WhatsApp" automation as one more on the list, configured just like the others. Without the add-on, that automation's gear icon opens a promotional window instead.

## Connect

Adds the **Connect** module (chat icon in the sidebar): the inbox where the clinic team reads and replies to their patients' WhatsApp messages, all centralized in one place, with filters by assignee, tags and quick replies to speed up the service.

You set it up from the gear icon inside Connect itself, in the "Getting started" tab: there you choose which location receives the WhatsApp messages, link the official line and decide which team members have access to the inbox. Optionally, you can also add a **virtual assistant** that replies on its own and hands off to the team when needed — it's a separate service within Connect.

## AI Integration

Adds a **conversational assistant** (at `/ai-assistant`) that you can ask in plain language about the clinic's data: billing, patients, schedule, clinician productivity, amounts owed by health insurers, treatment plans. The assistant searches the clinic's real data and answers on the spot, without you having to build a report by hand.

## DICOM Viewer

Lets you upload and view DICOM studies (CT scans) right in the patient's clinical record, from the **Media** tab. You upload the .zip or folder exported by the CT scanner, and the study opens in OCLÜ's own viewer with panoramic reconstruction and cross-sectional slices, without relying on an external program.

It's a separate service from regular Media (photos, videos, documents), which is included at no extra cost in the base plan.

## Online Booking

Gives the clinic a public link (to share on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere you like) so patients can book their own appointment, at any time, without calling. The patient chooses a clinician, a day and a free time slot, and the appointment goes straight into the calendar or waits for the clinic to approve it, depending on how it's set up.

You manage it from **Administration → Automations → Online booking**, where you'll see the active links, the requests waiting for approval and the booking statistics for each link. With the add-on active, a fourth island ("Online bookings") also appears above the calendar — see the **Calendar** module guide for the details.

## Electronic invoicing (Argentina)

Issues tax receipts integrated with AFIP directly from OCLÜ, without leaving the system. You reach it from the **Finance** dashboard, where the "Electronic invoicing" card appears with a shortcut.

## Integration API

Generates keys (API keys) to connect OCLÜ with external systems — n8n, Zapier, your own CRM — so you can automate workflows with the clinic's data. Each key can be limited by permissions (read or write) and revoked at any time. You manage it from **My settings › Integrations › Integration API** (see [My profile and settings](/en/mi-perfil)).

## Facial Harmonisation

An aesthetic diagnosis protocol inside the patient's clinical record: diagnosis profile with a chart of symmetry, shape and proportion, photo comparison and notes. It lives within FMS, in the patient's clinical record.

## Smile Design

A smile design studio: you load cases with the patient's photos, align them with AI and overlay them with the reference smile to plan the aesthetic treatment before starting it. Currently in beta.

## Spain Invoicing

OCLÜ's own invoicing tool for Spanish clinics, which issues legally valid invoices under Spanish regulations (RD 1619/2012). It's a completely separate module from Argentina's Electronic invoicing: it doesn't share numbering, template or integration — each country has its own tax workflow.

It's only available for clinics set up with Spain as their country, so we can't show a screenshot from an Argentine clinic like the one used in this guide.

### If you don't see some of these add-ons

If a service doesn't appear in your OCLÜ, it isn't an error: it means you haven't signed up for it yet. Message us through the **Support** button at the bottom of the screen or through **Contact** (three-dot menu) to talk to the team and add it to your plan.
