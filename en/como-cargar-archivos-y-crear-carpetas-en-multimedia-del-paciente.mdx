title: "How to upload files and create folders in the patient's Media"
---

The **Media** section of the patient's record is where you keep all the visual material and documents related to their care: X-rays, clinical photos (before/after), digital scans, videos and signed documents. This guide shows you how to upload files, create folders to organize them, and use the filters to quickly find what you're looking for.

## When to use it

- Every time you take an X-ray or clinical photo of the patient and want to save it in their record.
- When you receive an external study (CT scan, MRI, etc.) and need to file it under the patient.
- When you want a digital record of a signed consent form or any other patient document.
- When you're preparing a referral and want to organize all the relevant files in a dedicated folder.
- When you need to put together a visual case with the patient's photos to show them their treatment plan (see **Presentations** below).

> Media storage is **unlimited**, whatever plan you have.

## How to get to this section

1. Log in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the Patients icon.
3. Find the patient and click their name.
4. In the record, click the **Clinical record** tab.
5. In the side submenu, click **Media**.

## What the Media screen shows

When you open Media, you'll see:

- Two tabs: **Files** (the one shown by default) and **Presentations** (see below).
- A **search box** (**Search by name**) at the top left.
- A **funnel** icon to filter by category.
- Six **file type filter icons**: all, image, document, 3D, video and ID. Click them to filter the view.
- At the top right, the **New** dropdown and a **settings** icon (see the buttons section below).
- A central area with **two separate boxes** for uploading content: **Upload files** (images, PDFs, videos or 3D models, by dragging and dropping or with the **browse** link) and **DICOM files**, designed specifically for the **.zip or folder exported by the CT scanner**.
- Further down are the folders you've already created and the list of uploaded files, grouped by date.

> The **DICOM file viewer** is an add-on — an extra service that isn't included in the plan by default (see the **Add-ons** page). Since it's a different format, its storage is handled separately from the unlimited space for the rest of Media.

## How to upload files

### Option A: Drag and drop

1. On your computer, open the folder where the file you want to upload is.
2. Drag it from your computer onto the **Upload files** box on the Media screen.
3. Drop the file. OCLÜ starts the upload and, when it's done, adds it to the list.

### Option B: File picker

1. Click the **browse** link in the **Upload files** box.
2. Your operating system's file picker opens.
3. Choose one or more files and confirm.
4. OCLÜ uploads them and adds them to the list.

> You can upload several files at once by selecting multiple files at the same time in the picker, or by dragging a selection.

### Uploading a DICOM study

This is a separate action, with its own box so it doesn't get mixed up with regular files. Drag or select the .zip or folder exported by the CT scanner in the **DICOM files** box. Once processed, the study opens in OCLÜ's own 3D viewer, without relying on any external program.

## How to create a folder

Folders help you organize files by type or by stage of treatment (for example: "Initial X-rays", "Before/after photos", "External studies").

1. Click the black **New** button at the top right and choose **New folder**.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-481.png)
</Frame>

2. A form opens where you type the folder's name.
3. Confirm. The folder appears in the list.

Now you can upload files directly into that folder by opening it first.

## How to organize with Categories

**Categories** are labels you apply to each file to classify it (for example: "Panoramic X-ray", "Clinical photo", "Legal document"). Unlike folders, they are labels that travel with the file.

To create a new category, use **New → New category**. To manage the existing ones (edit or delete), use the **settings** icon at the top right → **Manage categories**.

When you upload a file, you can assign it one or more categories. Afterwards, the funnel icon (**Filter by category**) lets you quickly find all the files of one type (for example, all of the patient's panoramic X-rays).

## How to find files quickly

Over time, a patient's Media section can hold dozens of files. To find what you need:

- **Search box:** type part of the file name.
- **Type filters (icons):** click the icon for the type you want (image, document, 3D, video, ID). Only files of that type are shown.
- **Filter by category (funnel icon):** choose one of the categories your clinic has set up.

Filters can be combined: for example, you can filter "images" \+ "Panoramic X-ray category" to see only the panoramic ones.

Files can also be sorted: open the **settings** icon at the top right to choose between **Upload date** or **Capture date**, and **A → Z** or **Z → A** as the tie-breaker. From that same menu you can also **Share everything** in Media with a link.

## Editing and annotating images

Without leaving the system, every image has two tools:

- **Photo editor** — crops and adjusts the image.
- **Annotator** — used for marking up both photos and studies. It includes a palette with the odontogram symbols so you can point out findings directly on the image, and the zoom and any text you add can be freely repositioned.

## Presentations

Besides individual files, Media has a tab dedicated to building **presentations**: visual cases with the patient's photos to show them their treatment plan during the appointment.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-322.png)
</Frame>

Each presentation is built slide by slide. For each one you choose a layout (one image, two in columns, or combinations of photo and text) and a background color, and you add **photo** blocks — from the patient's gallery, which you can annotate — or free **text** blocks.

  ![Image](/images/image-323.png)

Once it's ready, the presentation can be shown full screen or shared with a public link, so it can be viewed from outside the system.

## Expected result

- Uploaded files stay permanently available in the patient's record.
- From their personal portal, the patient can also see the Media content linked to them.
- Any staff member with access to the patient's record can view the uploaded files.

## Common errors

**I uploaded a file but it doesn't appear in the list:** refresh the page. If it still doesn't appear, the upload may have failed (file too large, unsupported format, unstable connection). Try again.

**The file won't upload:** check its size and format. Very large files can take a while or fail. If it's an image, compress it before uploading.

**I can't find a file I uploaded a while ago:** use the search box with its name, or filter by type and by category. If you uploaded it without assigning a category, it will appear in the general list.

**I want to delete a file:** in the list, each file has actions available (depending on your permissions). If you don't see the delete option, check with your administrator.

## Tips and variations

- **Categorize when you upload:** assigning the right category as soon as you upload a file saves you a lot of time later when you look for it.
- **Folders to organize by stage:** "Initial", "During treatment" and "Final" usually work well for long treatments like orthodontics.
- **Before/after photos:** organize clinical photos in a "Comparisons" folder with clear names (e.g. "2024-03-15-before.jpg", "2024-09-20-after.jpg") to make comparing them easier.
- **Legal documents:** if your clinic uses Media to store signed consent forms, create a specific "Legal documents" category so you can find them easily.

## Estimated time

10 to 30 seconds to upload a file (depending on its size). 1 minute to create and name a new folder.

## Permissions

Uploading files, creating folders and managing categories require specific permissions assigned from Administration → Staff → Permissions. If a button appears disabled or you can't upload files, check with your administrator.
