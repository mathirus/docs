# Glossary of Terms

Definitions of terms used in OCLÜ and in dentistry.

---

## A

**Appointment** Time reserved in the calendar to see a patient. It has a date, time, duration and reason.

**Assistant** The dentist's assistant, who helps during appointments and handles basic administrative tasks.

---

## B

**Badge** A small label with a number that shows how many items there are. Example: a badge with "3" means there are 3 pending notifications.

**Balance** A financial summary that shows how much a patient owes, how much they have paid and how much they still have to pay.

---

## C

**Calendar** The view that shows all the booked appointments by day or week.

**Calendar block** (break) A time slot blocked so no appointments can be booked in it. Example: lunch, a meeting, a training session.

**Cash account** A place where money is recorded when you take a payment. It can be physical cash, a bank (a bank account) or a POS terminal (cards).

**Clinical note** A clinical entry that records what was done at an appointment. It describes the treatments carried out, observations and next steps.

**Clinical record** The full set of a patient's medical information: medical history, odontogram, clinical notes, media, prescriptions and consent forms.

**Consent form** (informed consent) A legal document the patient signs to authorize a treatment after learning about its risks and benefits.

**CUIT/CUIL** Argentina's unique tax (or employment) identification code. It is the tax ID used in Argentina.

---

## D

**Dashboard** (Home screen) The first screen you see when you sign in to the system. It shows a general overview and quick access to the modules.

**Deposit** (or advance payment) An initial payment the patient makes to confirm a treatment. Usually 20-50% of the total.

**Diagnosis** Identifying a patient's dental problems. Example: a cavity on tooth 16, a fracture on tooth 24.

**Digital prescription** A medical prescription generated electronically from the system. It includes medications, doses and instructions.

**DNI** Argentina's National Identity Document. The personal ID document in Argentina.

**Drag & Drop** Clicking an item, holding the button down and moving it to another position. Useful for rescheduling appointments in the calendar.

---

## E

**Expense** Money going out of the clinic. Example: paying salaries, buying supplies, services.

---

## F

**FDI** (FDI World Dental Federation) The international tooth numbering system. Adults: teeth 11 to 48. Children: teeth 51 to 85.

**FDI numbering** See "FDI".

**Filling** A treatment to repair a tooth with a cavity. It can be done with resin or amalgam.

**Finances** The system module that handles everything financial: cash accounts, payments, expenses, reports.

**Form** A set of fields you fill in to enter information into the system.

---

## H

**History** A chronological record of events or actions. Example: payment history, appointment history.

---

## I

**Implant** A titanium screw placed in the bone to replace the root of a missing tooth.

**Income** Money coming into the clinic, mainly from patient payments.

**Inventory** The list of dental materials and supplies available at the clinic, with their quantities.

---

## L

**Lab** (dental laboratory) An outside company that makes prostheses, crowns, bridges and other prosthetic work.

---

## M

**Media** The patient's files: intraoral photos, X-rays, videos, documents, 3D models.

**Medical history** A record of the illnesses, allergies, surgeries and medication a patient has had. Crucial information before any dental treatment.

**Modal** (pop-up window) A window that appears on top of the main screen. Usually used to fill in forms or confirm actions.

**MRN** (medical record number) A unique number that identifies each patient at your clinic. Example: MRN-001, MRN-002.

**Multi-tenant** An architecture where each clinic has its own isolated, secure space within the system.

---

## O

**Odontogram** A graphic representation of the patient's mouth that shows the condition of each tooth. It uses colors to indicate diagnoses and treatments.

**Organization** In OCLÜ, another word for "clinic" or "practice".

**Outstanding balance** The amount the patient still has to pay. It is calculated as: Treatment plan total - Payments made.

---

## P

**Patient** A person who receives dental care at your clinic.

**Payment method** The way the patient pays: cash, card, bank transfer, check, etc.

**Permission** Authorization to perform a specific action in the system. Example: "create patients", "view financial reports".

**Prosthesis** An artificial device that replaces missing teeth. It can be fixed (crowns, bridges) or removable (dentures).

---

## R

**Receipt** A document given to the patient as proof of a payment.

**Report** An analysis or set of statistics about some aspect of the clinic. Example: sales report, outstanding balances report.

**Role** A set of permissions that defines what a user can do. Examples: Super Admin, Doctor, Secretary.

---

## S

**SaaS** (Software as a Service) Cloud software you access over the internet, without installing anything on your computer.

**Session** The period of time you are connected to the system, from when you sign in until you sign out.

**Sign in** (Login) The process of entering the system with your username and password.

**Stock** The quantity of a material or supply available in the inventory.

---

## T

**Template** A predefined document format that is customized for each patient. Examples: medical prescription, consent form, treatment plan comment.

**Tenant** In a multi-tenant architecture, each clinic is a "tenant" with its own private, independent space.

**Toast** (pop-up notification) A temporary message that appears on screen to tell you about an action. Example: "Patient created successfully".

**Tooltip** (contextual help) Short text that appears when you hover over an item, explaining what it does.

**Tooth** Each of the teeth in the mouth. They are identified by numbers according to the FDI system.

**Tooth surface** Each of the faces of a tooth:

- **Mesial**: Front-facing surface (toward the center of the mouth)
- **Distal**: Back-facing surface (toward the wisdom tooth)
- **Occlusal**: Chewing surface (the top of lower teeth)
- **Buccal**: Outward-facing surface (toward the lip or cheek)
- **Lingual/Palatal**: Inward-facing surface (toward the tongue or palate)

**Treatment** A specific dental procedure. Examples: filling, root canal, extraction, crown, cleaning.

**Treatment plan** A document that details which treatments the patient needs, how much they cost and the agreed payment terms.

**Treatment rooms** The operatories or treatment rooms in the clinic. In the calendar, each treatment room can have its own column.

---

## U

**URL** The web address used to access the system. Example: `https://yourname.oclu.com`

**User** A person who has access to the system. Identified by an email address and a password.

---

## V

**Validation** An automatic check the system runs to make sure the data entered is correct. Example: checking that an email address has the format "[name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com)".

**View** A way of displaying information. Example: the calendar's daily view, weekly view.

---

## W

**Web App** (web application) An application that runs directly in the web browser, without installing any software.

**Wizard** A form split into several steps that guides you through a process. Example: the 3-step wizard for creating a treatment plan.

---

## Simple Technical Terms

**Browser** A program for viewing web pages. Examples: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari.

**Cache** The browser's temporary memory, which stores information so pages load faster. Sometimes you need to clear it if something isn't working.

**Cookies** Small files the browser saves to remember your preferences and session.

**Zoom** How far the screen is zoomed in or out. 100% is the normal size.
