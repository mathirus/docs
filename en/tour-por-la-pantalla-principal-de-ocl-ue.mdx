The first time you open OCLÜ, it can feel like a lot of information at once. In reality, the screen is organized in a very structured way and, once you understand where everything is, you move around quickly. This guide takes you through the four main areas: the left sidebar, the top bar, the home dashboard and the footer.

## When to use it

- When it's your first day using OCLÜ and you want to find your way around.
- When you're onboarding a new staff member and want to explain where everything is.
- When someone asks you where a feature is and you need to remember its location.

## How to get to the main screen

When you log in to OCLÜ, the system automatically takes you to the **Dashboard** (also called **Home**), which is the main screen. If you're somewhere else in the system, you can always go back Home from the first icon in the sidebar or from the breadcrumb at the top.

### Area 1: Left sidebar (the system's modules)

This is the narrow strip you see on the left side, with seven icons stacked one above the other. It's the main navigation menu. Each icon takes you to a different module:

1. **Calendar** (calendar icon): the daily view of appointments. This is where you book appointments, see the day's schedule and manage blocks.
2. **Patients** (people icon): the full list of patients. From here you open anyone's record and see their clinical record, treatment plans and balance.
3. **Laboratory** (clipboard icon): the module for managing requests to external labs.
4. **Stock** (box icon): the inventory of products, supplies and materials.
5. **Finances** (column icon): the financial module, with cash accounts, income, expenses, balance and reports.
6. **Connect** (chat icon): the inbox where you read and reply to WhatsApp messages with the clinic's patients. It's an add-on — if your clinic hasn't subscribed to it, you won't see this icon.
7. **Administration** (people-with-arrow icon): the system settings. This is where you set up clinics, staff, treatments, templates, automations and advanced reports.

> If you hover over the sidebar, it expands automatically and you'll see the full name of each module. It helps you identify each icon when you're just starting out.

> **Connect**'s settings (which location receives the WhatsApp messages, which number is linked, who on the team handles them) aren't in Administration: you open them with the gear icon inside the Connect module itself.

### Area 2: Top bar

This is the dark strip you see at the top of the screen. From left to right, it has the following elements:

- **OCLÜ logo \+ ▼ arrow** (on the left): the clinic selector. If your organization has several clinics, click it to see the list and choose which one to switch to. There's a dedicated guide: **How to switch between clinics**.
- **Search (magnifying glass icon):** the system-wide search.
- **Bell (notifications):** your pending reminders and system news (improvements, new features). The number shows how many new ones there are.
- **Envelope (messages):** quick access to the staff's internal messaging.
- **User name** (on the right): opens a menu with **My settings** (edit your profile), **My subscription** (administrators only: plan, limits and billing), **My fees** (doctors with a schedule and a contract set up) and **Log out**.

### Area 3: Dashboard / Central area (Home)

This is what you see in the middle of the screen when you log in. All the information is organized in **islands** — independent blocks, each focused on one area of the clinic — that you can rearrange by dragging them however you like:

- **Appointments island:** the full list of today's appointments, with time, patient, linked treatment plan, description and status. It can be filtered by doctor.
- **Calendar island:** how many appointments are booked today and which one is next.
- **Patients island:** today's birthdays.
- **Finances island:** the monthly balance, with an eye icon to hide the amounts.
- **Laboratory island:** how many lab requests are due today.
- **Reminders island:** internal staff tasks — these aren't appointment reminders, they're tasks like "Call patient X" or "Offer a check-up to patient Y". Each one has a date, a linked patient (optional), a description, a category and actions.
- **Personal notes island:** your own private notes board — nobody else sees them.
- **Messages island:** the staff's internal messaging, optionally linked to a patient.

This guide gives you the big picture; for the full details of each island (what it shows, how to use it, every field in its forms) see the **Interface and Navigation** guide.

### Area 4: Footer

This is the dark strip at the bottom of the screen, present on every screen of the system. From left to right:

- **Logo and copyright:** "OCLÜ · © 2026 · by Dr. Fernando M. Soto".
- **Support (WhatsApp button):** opens WhatsApp with the team for any question — technical problems as well as sales or administrative matters. It's the same number you'll find as "Sales, admin and support" in the **Contact** window of the three-dot menu.

## Suggested tour for your first day

1. **Start with the Dashboard:** spend 5 minutes understanding what each island shows.
2. **Go through the sidebar:** open each module (Calendar, Patients, Laboratory, Stock, Finances, Connect if you have it) without entering anything. Just look at each one's main screen.
3. **Look at the top bar:** click the bell, the envelope, your name and the three-dot menu to see what's in each one.
4. **Try switching modes:** from the three-dot menu, switch between light and dark mode to see which one you're more comfortable with.
5. **Check out OclüLearn and the User guide:** both are in the three-dot menu — OclüLearn is video tutorials, and the User guide is this same written documentation.

## Common errors

**I don't see all the icons in the sidebar:** check your permissions. Some staff members may have restricted access to certain modules (for example, a receptionist without access to Finances).

**The Administration module doesn't expand:** click it instead of hovering. The sidebar sometimes expands on hover, but the submenus need a click.

**I see only a few items on the Dashboard:** it depends on the day's activity. If there are no appointments, reminders or pending messages, the islands may appear empty. It's not an error.

## Tips and variations

- **Learn the icons:** for the first few weeks you'll hover to see the names. After that you'll recognize them just by the shape of the icon and move around faster.
- **Set light/dark mode to your liking:** from day one. Many people prefer dark mode for long sessions.
- **Use the Dashboard as your starting point:** when you start the day, before doing anything else, look at the Dashboard. It shows you what's pending (appointments, reminders, messages).
- **The clinic selector is always at the top left:** remember to check it before making important changes so you don't work in the wrong location.

## Estimated time

10 to 15 minutes for a full tour on your first day.

## Permissions

What you see in the sidebar and on the Dashboard depends on your permissions. Some modules may be hidden depending on the staff member's role. If you expect to see something and it doesn't appear, check with your administrator from Administration → Staff → Permissions.
