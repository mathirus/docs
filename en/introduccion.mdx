# What is OCLÜ?

_by Fernando M. Soto_

<Frame>
  ![Introduction](/images/Introducción.png)
</Frame>

**OCLÜ** is management software for dental clinics, built specifically to support the day-to-day work of the practice. It runs entirely in the cloud, which means there's nothing to install: you sign in from any browser, at any time, from any device with an internet connection.

---

## Cloud software

OCLÜ being cloud software isn't a technical detail — it's a choice that directly affects how you work every day.

- **No installation needed** — All you need is an up-to-date browser and an internet connection. Nothing else.
- **Automatic updates** — You'll always have the latest version available, with no interruptions or manual steps.
- **Real-time sync** — All information updates instantly. If a secretary records a payment, the doctor sees it at that very moment from another computer.
- **Access from multiple devices** — You can work from the office computer, a laptop or a tablet. The information is always the same, no matter where you sign in from.
- **_Companion mobile app_** — Available for Android and iOS for access from your phone.

**Compatibility**: OCLÜ works on desktop computers, laptops and tablets. On phones, we recommend using the mobile app for a better experience.

**Supported browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari.

---

## Availability

OCLÜ is built for the Spanish-speaking market and is available to clinics in Latin America and Europe. The system's full functionality is 100% available in Argentina. We'll gradually add country-specific features for other countries, adapted to the local rules and regulations of each region.

---

## What makes OCLÜ special?

### Multiple clinics, separate information

OCLÜ lets you manage several locations or clinics within the same platform, keeping each one's information completely separate. Each clinic has its own space, its own team, its own patients, payments, treatments and price lists. The only thing shared between locations is the patient list, so if a patient is seen at more than one branch, their information is available without having to enter them twice.

This means you can have multiple clinics in a single account and, at the same time, each one works completely independently and can be customized.

### Fully integrated

Everything in OCLÜ is connected. A recorded payment is automatically reflected in the patient's balance. A clinical note updates the odontogram. An approved treatment plan can create a lab request. An appointment can record that day's payment. There's no need to enter the same information twice or jump between systems.

### Built for dentists

OCLÜ isn't generic software adapted to dentistry. It was designed from clinical practice, with dental terminology, real clinical workflows and specific tools such as the interactive odontogram, the 3D viewer and lab management.
