---
title: "How to create a patient"
---

Before you book an appointment, record a treatment plan or enter any clinical data, the patient has to exist in OCLÜ. This guide shows you the two ways to add them: by hand, or by letting the patient fill in their own details from a link.

## When to use it

- When a new patient arrives at the clinic and you need to register them.
- When a prospective patient gets in touch through WhatsApp or social media and you want them to register themselves, without you having to type anything.
- When you need to enter the basic details quickly to book them an appointment.
- When you're completing a patient's record with all their contact and address details.
- When you want the patient to have access to their own portal to see treatment plans, clinical notes or media.
- When you want to know the patient's full address (street, country, province, city).

## How to get to this section

1. Log in to OCLÜ.
2. In the left sidebar, click the second icon (a group of people). It takes you to the **Patients** list.
3. At the top right, click the black **Add patient** button. A dropdown opens with two options.

- **Manually** ("Enter the details yourself"): opens the two-step form you'll see further down in this guide.
- **Share link** ("Let the patient fill in their own details"): creates a link (and a QR code) so the patient can register themselves from their phone, without you typing anything.

> In the breadcrumb at the top, if you choose Manually, you'll see **Home → Patients → Add**.

## Quick option: let the patient register themselves

If you choose **Share link**, the **Share registration link** window opens, with two modes:

- **Single use:** a one-time link to invite a specific patient. It stops working as soon as the patient completes it, and it expires 7 days after it was created if it isn't used.
- **Permanent:** a reusable link, meant to be published (for example in your Instagram bio or on the clinic's WhatsApp) so anyone can register.

Copy the link with the **Copy** button, or share the QR code generated below it directly.

The patient fills in their details from their own phone and is registered in the system, without you having to enter anything manually.

## Steps to add a patient manually

The form has two steps: **Basic details** and **Additional details**. An indicator at the top shows which one you're on.

### Step 1: Basic details

Fill in the fields. The ones marked with a red asterisk (`*`) are required.

**First name** (required): the patient's first name.

**Surname** (required): the full surname.

**Email** (optional): the patient's email address. Even though the field isn't required, it's best to add it whenever it's available: without an email you won't be able to send them notifications, appointment reminders, links to update their details or digital prescriptions.

**Date of birth** (required): DD/MM/YYYY format. You can type it directly or use the calendar that appears when you click the field.

**Gender** (required): choose **Male**, **Female** or **Undefined** from the dropdown.

**Country code** (required): set to **\+54 (Argentina)** by default. If the patient has a number from another country, change it from the dropdown.

**Phone** (required): the number without the country code (you already selected it above). For example, if the full number is \+54 11 1234-5678, type 1112345678.

**ID type** (optional): DNI by default. Change it if the patient uses another type of ID.

**ID number** (required): the number for the type you chose. The field accepts any format.

**Medical record number** (optional): if your clinic uses internal record codes (for example HC-001, HC-2024-045), enter the number here. The form shows you the last number you used, for reference. If you don't use them, leave it empty — the system gives the patient an internal ID anyway.

**Upload photo** (optional): on the left there's a box to upload a photo of the patient. It's useful for recognizing them visually in their record.

Once all the required fields are filled in, click the black **Next step** button at the bottom right. If a required field is missing, OCLÜ points it out with a red message under the field and a notification in the top right corner; fix it and try again.

### Step 2: Additional details

This screen has three sections: **Address**, **Health insurer** and **Additional information**.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-473.png)
</Frame>

**Address** (all fields required):

- **Street address:** street and number, plus apartment if it applies.
- **Country of residence:** choose from the dropdown.
- **Province:** enter the province (e.g. Buenos Aires, Córdoba).
- **City or town:** enter the city or town (e.g. La Plata, Villa Crespo).

**Health insurer** (all fields optional):

- **Health insurer:** choose from the dropdown if the patient has coverage. Your clinic sets up the list of options from Administration.
- **Insurance plan:** the specific plan (e.g. 210, Plata, Premium).
- **Member number:** the patient's member number with the insurer.
- **Referred by:** if someone referred them (another patient, another clinician, a campaign), note it here. Useful for later analysis of where your patients come from.

**Additional information** (optional):

- **First visit date:** if you already know when this patient's first visit was or will be, enter it here (there are tabs so you can fill in only the Day, Month or Year if you don't know the full date).

When you're done, click the black **Add patient** button at the bottom right. (If you need to fix something from step 1, click **Previous step**.)

## Expected result

OCLÜ saves the patient and takes you straight to their personal record. In the breadcrumb you'll see **Home → Patients → \[Surname Name\]**. From there you can:

- Enter their medical history in the **Clinical record** tab.
- Create a treatment plan from the **Treatment plans** tab.
- See the patient's financial balance in **Balance**.
- Book them an appointment from the calendar.
- Send them the **Patient link** so they can access their portal.

The patient also appears in the general Patients list. If you left any optional field empty (for example email), they'll count in the list's **Incomplete** filter until you fill it in.

## Common errors

**"Gender is required" / "Phone number is required" / etc.:** a field from step 1 is missing. Look at the red messages under each field and fix them.

**Duplicate ID number:** if you enter an ID number that already belongs to another patient, the system warns you. Check whether the patient is already in the system by searching the list by ID number.

**I can't move on to step 2:** there's most likely a validation pending in step 1. Scroll up and check whether any field is marked with an error.

**I can't find the patient's health insurer in the list:** each clinic sets up its own list of insurers from Administration. If one is missing, add it from there or ask your administrator.

**I entered the phone with the country code and it shows up twice:** the Phone field expects the number WITHOUT the country code (the country code goes in the separate Country code field). Edit it and delete the extra digits.

## Tips and variations

- **If the patient hasn't arrived yet and you need to book their appointment:** add them with whatever minimum details you have and complete the rest later. They'll show up in the Incomplete filter until everything is filled in.
- **To invite a specific patient without typing anything:** use **Add patient → Share link** in **Single use** mode and send them the link or the QR code by WhatsApp.
- **To have a fixed registration link** (for example on social media): use the **Permanent** mode in the same window.
- **To add patients in bulk:** use **Import / Export → Import patients** in the Patients list. The system has separate imports: one for patients and another for clinical notes of patients already in the system.
- **Patient photo:** the image you upload appears in their record and in the calendar next to their name. Useful for recognizing them visually, especially in large clinics.

## Estimated time

2 to 3 minutes per patient when you enter all the details by hand. A few seconds if you use the self-registration link.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I create a patient without an email?** Yes, email is optional. But without an email you can't send them appointment notifications, reminders, update links or digital prescriptions. It's best to add it whenever it's available.

**Can I edit the patient's details after creating them?** Yes. In the Patients list, click the patient's name to open their record. In **Personal details → Information** you can change every field and save with the **Save changes** button.

**What's the difference between the ID number and the Medical record number?** The **ID number** is the patient's national ID, passport or other identity document — an external piece of data that identifies them officially. The **Medical record number** is an internal code each clinic uses to organize its files or records. The system also assigns an automatic internal ID; all three are different.

**What if the patient has no fixed address or doesn't want to give it?** The address is required. At a minimum, enter the country and province. If you don't know the exact address, you can type "Not provided" in the Street address field and complete it later.

**Can I upload a photo straight from the camera?** The **Upload photo** box accepts files from your device. If you're working from your phone, you can use the camera and the photo is uploaded to the box.

**What happens if the single-use registration link expires before the patient uses it?** You need to create a new one from **Add patient → Share link**. Single-use links expire after 7 days.

## Permissions

Creating and editing patients requires specific permissions in Administration → Staff → Permissions. If you don't see the **Add patient** button or can't edit fields, check the settings with your system administrator.
