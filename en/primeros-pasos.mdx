This section walks you through signing up and accessing OCLÜ for the first time, from creating your account to signing in to the system.

## How do I sign up for OCLÜ?

There are three ways to start the sign-up process:

- **From the website** — Go to the OCLÜ website and click **Request a demo**. The button is visible as soon as you open the site and stays fixed in the top-right corner. When you click it, the registration form opens directly.
- **Through the sales team** — You can get in touch with the sales team and they'll share the link to the registration form with you.
- **After a demo meeting** — If you want to get to know the system before signing up, you can request a presentation meeting with the OCLÜ team. The demo lasts between 30 and 60 minutes. Once you decide to move forward, they'll share the form with you.

> To contact the sales team: [**info@oclucrm.com**](mailto:info@oclucrm.com) / +54 9 11 3011-6440

## Registration form

Registration is self-service and has four steps.

### Step 1: User

The details of the clinic's main administrator: this user has full control of the system; their permissions can't be restricted and their access can't be disabled.

Fields to fill in:

- First and last name
- Email _(this will be your sign-in username)_
- Country
- Main currency
- Phone, with a country code selector
- ID / Document
- Date of birth _(optional)_
- Password and confirmation — at least 9 characters and one uppercase letter

> If the email you entered already has clinics registered in OCLÜ, the system lets you know and suggests signing in instead of creating a new account.

### Step 2: Verification

The system sends a 6-digit code to the email you entered in the previous step. You enter it to confirm the account is yours and move on to the next step. If it doesn't arrive, you can ask for the code to be resent after a short wait.

### Step 3: Clinic

The clinic's basic details:

- Clinic name
- Clinic email
- Phone
- Full address
- Logo _(optional)_

### Step 4: Finish

You choose the custom domain you'll use to access the system and accept the terms and conditions. The URL will have this format:

```text
yourchoice.oclucrm.com
```

> **Important** — This is your clinic's unique address in OCLÜ. Save it, because you'll need it every time you want to sign in to the system. Type it into the browser exactly as you wrote it, without the `www.`

## Signing in for the first time

Once you've completed the form, you can sign in to the system:

1. Open your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari)
2. Enter your custom URL (`yourchoice.oclucrm.com`)
3. You'll see the OCLÜ sign-in screen
4. Fill in your **email** and your **password**
5. Click **Sign in**

---

## I forgot my password

If you don't remember your password, there are three ways to recover or reset it:

### From the sign-in screen

If you can't get into the system because you don't remember your password, you can reset it directly from the sign-in screen:

1. Go to your custom URL (`yourchoice.oclucrm.com`)
2. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**
3. The password recovery screen opens
4. Enter the email you're registered with in the system
5. Click **Send instructions**
6. Check your email and follow the steps to create a new password

> If you can't find the recovery email, check your spam or junk folder. The email is sent to the address registered in the system, so it's important that it's an inbox you have access to. If the email doesn't arrive, contact the support or sales team for help.

### From the Administration module

If you're the clinic's administrator and need to reset the password of a member of your team, you can do it without the user having to do it from the sign-in screen:

1. Go to the **Administration** module
2. Open the **Staff** section
3. Find the staff member
4. Click the padlock icon in the actions column of their row
5. Choose one of the two options:
   - **Send them an email** — the user gets a link and chooses the new password themselves. Nobody else knows it.
   - **Set it myself** — you type it in and pass it on to them some other way. Useful if they don't have access to their email.

## Tips for a secure password

To create a secure password, make sure it has:

- At least **9 characters**
- At least **one uppercase letter**
- At least **one number**
- Special characters (!@#\$%) for extra security
- Avoid personal details such as your first name, last name or date of birth
- Don't share it with anyone, not even the support team

## What does being the main administrator mean?

The main administrator is the owner of the system within the clinic. They have full access to every feature and their permissions can't be restricted. From their account they can:

- Create, edit and manage all the team's users
- Set up the clinic and its operating settings
- Assign permissions to the rest of the staff
- Access all financial and clinical information

**Main administrator details you can edit:**

- ✅ First and last name
- ✅ ID document
- ✅ Email
- ✅ Phone
- ✅ Password
- ✅ Profile photo
- ❌ Role _(it will always be Main administrator)_

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't get the confirmation email

1. Check your spam or junk folder
2. Make sure the email entered in the form is correct
3. Wait up to 30 minutes
4. If the problem continues, contact support

### I can't sign in with my credentials

1. Make sure the URL is spelled correctly and has no `www.`
2. Check that the email and password are correct
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try clearing the browser's cache and cookies
5. Try another browser

### I forgot my password

Follow the steps in the **I forgot my password** section above.
