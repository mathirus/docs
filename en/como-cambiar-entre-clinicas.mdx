---
title: "How to switch between clinics"
---

OCLÜ lets a single account have several clinics set up (for example, an organization with three locations in different cities). When you work with multiple clinics, some information is shared across all the locations (patients, for example) and some is specific to each clinic (the calendar, finances). This guide shows you how to switch between clinics and what information you see in each case.

## When to use it

- When your organization has several clinics in the same OCLÜ account.
- When you need to work on the calendar, treatment plans or finances of a location other than the one you have active.
- When you work as the administrator of several clinics and switch between them depending on the day's work.

## Prerequisites

- Your account needs to have access to more than one clinic.
- Additional clinics are added from **Administration → Clinics** by a user with permissions.
- Your user needs to be assigned to the clinics you want to see. Staff are assigned to clinics from **Administration → Staff → the staff member's actions menu**.

## How to get to this section

The clinic selector is always available at the top left of every screen in the system, next to the OCLÜ logo.

1. Sign in to OCLÜ.
2. At the top left, you'll see the name of the active clinic and a down arrow next to it.
3. Click there. A list opens with all the clinics your user has access to, with a check mark next to the active one.

## Steps to switch clinics

1. Click the arrow next to the active clinic's name.
2. In the list that opens, click the clinic you want to switch to.

That's it: there's no separate confirmation step. As soon as you click, OCLÜ reloads the session with the new active clinic and the name at the top left changes instantly.

## What is shared and what is specific to each clinic

This is the most important part for understanding how multi-clinic OCLÜ works.

### Information shared across all clinics

- **Patient list:** a patient added in any clinic appears in all of them. You don't have to add them twice if they're going to be seen at several locations.
- **Patient personal details:** first name, last name, ID, date of birth, contact details, address, health insurance. Everything is unique per patient.
- **Patient medical background:** the medical history, medications, habits and dental history entered in the Medical background section are shared across clinics.
- **Patient media:** the files uploaded in the Media section (X-rays, clinical photos, documents) are shared across clinics.

### Information specific to each clinic

- **Calendar and appointments:** each clinic has its own workspace. Appointments, schedules and assigned clinicians are specific to the active location.
- **Treatment plans:** each treatment plan belongs to the clinic where it was created. You can't see one location's treatment plans while you're in another.
- **Patient balance:** financial movements (charges, payments, voids) are per clinic. A patient can have different balances at each location.
- **Finances:** cash accounts, income, expenses, adjustments and financial reports are per clinic.
- **Stock (inventory):** each clinic keeps its own product inventory.
- **Lab (requests):** lab requests are per clinic.

### Staff and permissions

- **Administrators** usually have access to all the information of every clinic.
- The rest of the staff are assigned individually to the clinics where they'll work, from **Administration → Staff → the staff member's actions menu**. Each member can only access the locations they were assigned to.

## Expected result

As soon as you click the clinic in the list, OCLÜ reloads the session with the new active clinic. You'll see:

- The same patient list (with the same details, medical background and media).
- A different calendar, different treatment plans, a different balance for each patient, different cash accounts and financial reports, different stock and different lab requests.

## Common errors

**The list with several options doesn't appear:** your user probably only has access to one clinic. If you should have access to another one, ask your administrator to assign you from Administration → Staff.

**I switched clinics but I see the same patients:** that's normal. The patient list is shared across all clinics. What changes is the data specific to each location (treatment plans, balance, appointments, etc.).

**I see a patient added at another clinic that I didn't expect:** that's normal for the same reason. The patient list is global. Shared clinical data (medical background, media) is also visible, but treatment plans and balance only appear if you created them in this clinic.

**I can't find a clinic I expected to see in the list:** check that the clinic has been created in Administration → Clinics and that your user has permissions for it.

## Tips and variations

- **Name your clinics carefully:** give them clear names (for example "Oclü Palermo", "Oclü Recoleta") so you can tell them apart quickly in the list.
- **Before making important changes:** always check which clinic is active by looking at the name at the top left. Since switching is instant and has no confirmation, it's easy to add a treatment plan or record a payment in the wrong clinic if you're not paying attention.
- **Patients seen at several locations:** you don't need to duplicate the record. Keep a single record for the patient, and see them at each clinic with independent treatment plans and balances at each location.
- **Staff assignment:** when you bring a new member onto the team, assign them only the clinics where they'll work, to avoid unnecessary access.

## Estimated time

2 seconds to switch between two clinics.

## Permissions

Access to multiple clinics is set up from Administration → Staff. Administrators usually have access to all of them; the rest of the staff only to the clinics they were assigned to. If you need to work with more clinics but don't see them in the list, check with your administrator.
